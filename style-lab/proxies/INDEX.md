## How the no-face ranges were made
- Candidate windows came from a face detector (OpenCV Haar frontal + profile, sampled every 0.2 s). Every window was then checked by eye on 4 fps frame strips. Boundaries are accurate to about ±0.25 s.
- A range counts as *no face* when no part of the face above the chin is visible. Back-of-head views are included, as are close-ups cropped at the neck. Where a chin or lips touch the top edge, it says so.
- Three-quarter turns where a cheek or profile is visible are **excluded**.
- Cross-dissolve frames that still carry a ghost of the face are excluded, so ranges start or end about 0.25 s inside each dissolve.
