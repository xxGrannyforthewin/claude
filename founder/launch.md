# Launch plan: Bonita Style Houston, opening Sun Nov 1, 2026, 10 AM

Today is Sat Oct 24: 8 days out. The lease, build-out and stock are committed, so the "cheap test before you spend" step has changed. **Treat opening week as the real-world test.** Write down what success looks like now, and judge the store against that line, not against hope.

## 1. The real-world test (Nov 1–14)

**The success line, written before opening:**
- Opening day: **40+ orders** (the panel's 15–20% buy rate is an upper bound, so this checks real demand against it).
- Days 2–14: **an average of 6+ orders a day** (on the way to the 10/day break-even).
- **100+ Ramadan early-access sign-ups** by Nov 14.
- **15+ Google reviews** by Nov 14.

If real buyers fall well short, trust them over the panel. Go back to the offer (`offer.md`) and pricing (`pricing.md`) before spending more on ads.
