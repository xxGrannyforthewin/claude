# Marketing: Bonita Style Houston, opening Nov 1, 2026

This page builds on your existing Notion brief ("Bonita Style Content Brief — Oct 19 to Nov 15"). That brief already sets the content mix, UTM tags and Lives. This page adds what the panel and the competitor map found.

## 1. The evidence in one minute

- **Who buys (simulated panel, thin samples):** "must try it on" shoppers (2 of 3 bought), shoppers who already go to **Hillcroft** on weekends, and buyers with a **wedding coming up**. TikTok discoverers were next.
- **Who doesn't:** deal hunters anchored on $20–30 online abayas, loyal shoppers of a Hillcroft/Sugar Land boutique, women whose clothes come from family abroad, review-readers (the store has no Google rating yet), and South Asian shoppers who see "Gulf-style" and think it's not for them.
- **Biggest reason to pass: timing.** November has no Eid. But Ramadan starts around **Feb 8, 2027**, Eid al-Fitr is around **Mar 9–10, 2027**, and fall/winter is **wedding season**.
- **The gap (competitors):** local try-on of embroidered, occasion-ready pieces at **$45–$80**, between **Hayat's** ($40–65, Beechnut) and **Asli** ($90–199, Hwy 6 S, 5.0 from 500+ Google reviews). The top complaints about everyone else: **sheer fabric / not like the photo** and **sizing**.

## 2. Positioning

1. **(Recommended)** For modest women in southwest Houston with a wedding, henna night or Eid coming up, who've been burned by online abayas that came sheer, short or the wrong colour, **Bonita Style is the boutique where you try embroidered crepe on, get the length checked and take it home the same day, for $45–$80**, unlike online shops that ship and hope.
   *Evidence:* every buyer who bought did so for try-on and convenience. "Sheer / not like the photo / sizing" are the top competitor complaints.
2. For the 30K women who already watch Bonita on TikTok: **"the pieces you've been saving, now on a rack you can touch, 5 minutes from Hillcroft."** (Good for existing followers. Doesn't reach new Houston buyers.)
3. **"Southwest Houston's occasion fitting room."** (The board's framing. Strong, but abstract for a hook.)

## 4. Ten hooks (each answers a panel objection)

1. "hold it up to the window. that's how you know it's not see-through" (sheer fabric)
2. "5'9? we check the length on you before you pay" (fit/length)
3. "the $25 online abaya vs our $45 one, side by side" (price; show fabric, no trash-talking a named brand)
4. "wedding in december? walk out fitted today" (timing + same day)
5. "houston, you can finally try us on" (TikTok followers → store)
6. "5 minutes from hillcroft, gold racks, fitting rooms" (the drive)
7. "if it doesn't fit at home, exchange it free for 14 days" (risk)
8. "not shopping yet? get first pick of our ramadan pieces" (timing → list)
9. "the colour you saw on tiktok, under real light" (colour mismatch)
10. "we're not a pop-up. here's our lease-signing to opening day" (trust/"will it close?". Use your build-out footage)

*South Asian shoppers:* only feature colourful kaftans and sets (e.g. Flowers Kuftan) for "dholki and Eid" if they're actually on the rack. Don't promise a desi range you don't stock.

## 6. Budget and the numbers

- **Budget for the first 30 days:** about $150 printing + $0 organic + about $250 boosted posts from Nov 16 (rising to ~$500/mo, as in the CFO model).
- **Most you can pay to win a store customer:** each order leaves $42.09. The panel's buyers said about 1 purchase in the first month. **Keep the cost of a first purchase under about $20** (half the contribution), so the first order pays for itself. Repeat visits before Ramadan and Eid are where the profit comes from.
- **Three numbers to watch weekly:**
  1. **Orders per day** vs. the 10/day break-even. Under 6 for two weeks running means change the offer or the channels.
  2. **Source of each buyer** (register question + codes). If one channel brings under 10% after 3 weeks, cut its time.
  3. **Ramadan list sign-ups.** Target 300 by Jan 15. Under 100 by Dec 15 means push the list harder in Lives and at the register.
