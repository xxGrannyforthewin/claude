# Bonita Style Houston: one page

**The problem.** Modest women in southwest Houston buy occasion wear online and get burned: sheer fabric, wrong length, colours unlike the photo, and Eid orders that arrive late or not at all. Those are the top complaints across local and online competitors (competitors.md).

**The customer.** Modest women 18–55 in SW Houston (Westchase, Hillcroft, Sharpstown, Sugar Land) shopping for weddings, henna nights, Ramadan and Eid. Strongest fit: "must try it on" shoppers who already go to Hillcroft on weekends.

**The offer and price.** Embroidered crepe thobes $74.99–$79.99, everyday abayas $45 (2 for $80), wedding look thobe + bisht $149, hijabs $10. **The Bonita Fit Promise:** length checked on you in the fitting room, free 14-day fit exchange. Opening day 15% off (Nov 1). Free hijab with a thobe (Nov 2–8). Ramadan early-access list.

**Verdict (computed): Not yet.** Year 1 loses money on the current estimates, and the panel buy rate is under the 25% bar.

**The evidence.**
- Panel: every simulated buyer who bought did so for try-on and convenience. Those who passed cited November timing, $25 online abayas, loyalty to existing shops and no reviews yet.
- Competitor gap: no local store clearly sells embroidered occasion pieces in the $45–$80 band, between Hayat's ($40–65) and Asli ($90–199).
- Real proof starts Nov 1: the success line is 40 orders on opening day and 6+ a day after.

**What the money is for.** Finishing the build-out (sign, chandeliers, packaging, POS, cameras: about $16,700 still unpaid on the sheet) and carrying the store through a slow November–January to the Ramadan and Eid peak (Feb–Mar 2027).

**The ask (to the owner).** Confirm the landed cost per piece and real wages, then choose two levers: a $95 average order and 2 paid staff on weekdays. Together they bring break-even down to 7 orders a day and year 1 to about even (-$1,900) on the same cautious ramp.
