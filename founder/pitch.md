Bonita Style, a modest boutique, opens its first store on Sunday, November 1, 2026, at 8401 Westheimer Rd, Suite 130, Houston (near Westchase, 5 minutes from Hillcroft).
Embroidered crepe thobes ($74.99 to $79.99), everyday abayas ($45, or 2 for $80), kaftans, jalabiya sets ($119.99) and chiffon hijabs ($10). Fabrics are named on every tag, and you can feel them before you buy.
The Bonita Fit Promise: staff check the length on you in the fitting room, and if it doesn't fit right at home, exchange it free within 14 days.
Opening day, Nov 1: 15% off everything. Nov 2 to 8: a free matching chiffon hijab with any thobe.
Wedding-season look: any thobe plus the Layla Bisht for $149 (separately about $175).
