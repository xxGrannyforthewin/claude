## What would flip a no

- If friends or family I trust wore their pieces to a wedding or henna night and the embroidery and fabric really stood out, I'd go and look before Eid.
- Seeing actual photos of colorful or less traditional everyday pieces, plus a real sale bringing an everyday abaya down to about $30, maybe shipped or online so I don't have to drive out there.
- A wedding invitation in the next few weeks, plus photos showing their thobes and the Layla Bisht look are more modern and distinctive than what I already own.
- Seeing a TikTok or Live from them that shows the embroidery and the crepe up close, next to a cheap online abaya, right before a wedding I actually have to dress for. Then the 2-for-$80 abayas or a thobe with the free hijab might get me to stop in.
- A wedding or family event coming up in November. Then the fit check in the fitting room and the free 14-day exchange would get me in to try on a thobe.
- A TikTok or Instagram Live from the store showing colourful, heavily embroidered pieces that suit South Asian taste, like kaftans or sets I could wear to a mehndi, styled on women who look like me.
- An invite to a wedding or henna night this month. Then the Fit Promise and the thobe-plus-bisht deal would get me to buy there.
- A last-minute need, like a wedding or henna night coming up before my relatives' parcel arrives, and a friend telling me the crepe is thick and lined. Then I'd go for the thobe plus Layla Bisht deal.
- A real deal on the 2-for abayas, like 2 for $60, or the 15% off plus the free hijab running the same weekend, would get me to drive over and feel the crepe myself. If it's actually thick and not see-through, I'd buy.
- Seeing a solid Google Maps rating with real customer photos of the pieces, especially ones that look modern and not too plain.
- Seeing them do a TikTok or Instagram Live where they show the fabric up close and let me order online with that free exchange, especially if the 2 for $80 abayas could ship to me instead of me driving across town.
- Seeing the actual pieces, if there is something genuinely different and modern for a wedding or henna night that I can't find at the Hillcroft shops, and an invitation coming up that I need it for.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
