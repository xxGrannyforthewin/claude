## What would flip a no

- If my friends showed up in Bonita thobes at a wedding or henna night and the embroidery looked better than what my usual shop carries, I'd make the drive to see it.
- Seeing the embroidered thobes in person and finding the crepe heavy and well finished, with lengths that fit me without hemming, especially if a family wedding comes up.
- Seeing pieces in colors and cuts that fit my taste, at a real sale price on the everyday abayas (around $30), would get me to stop by.
- A wedding or henna night on my calendar in the next few weeks, plus seeing that the pieces are modern and dressy, not just basic abayas.
- A Live or video showing the fabric and stitching up close next to a cheap online abaya, plus a deal on an everyday abaya and hijab together, or a family event coming up that I need an outfit for.
- A wedding or event coming up soon, or the same kind of discount and fitting help running in the weeks before Ramadan.
- Seeing a TikTok or Live from them with colourful embroidered pieces or kaftans styled the way desi aunties dress for Eid and dholkis, at under $60.
- If I had a wedding or Eid coming up and they had pieces in a proper long length that I could try on and take home the same day, with the 15% off still going.
- Touching the fabric and finding it heavy, opaque and soft, with proper embroidery, right before a family wedding or Eid when I don't have time to get something sent from home.
- A real sale I can't get online, like everyday abayas around $30 or a buy-two deal, plus seeing in person that the fabric is thick and not scratchy. I'd maybe go closer to Eid or a wedding when I need something nicer anyway.
- A few months of solid Google Maps reviews with customer photos showing modern, less traditional cuts, or a friend from the masjid wearing something from there that I actually like.
- If they did a TikTok Live showing the embroidery and crepe up close next to a cheap online abaya, with a real deal on the $45 everyday abaya I could order without driving across town.
