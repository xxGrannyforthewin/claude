# Brand: Bonita Style Houston

**The name and identity are already set. Don't rename.** Bonita Style, "Modest Boutique": watercolor wordmark, maroon and gold on white, natural-light UGC look (from your General Content Brief). A store four years into the brand (California LLC since 2022) with 30K TikTok followers shouldn't start over.

## What the store adds

- **One-line promise:** *Try it on. Get the length right. Wear it this week.*
- **Tagline for the window and bags:** *Bonita Style · Modest Boutique · Try it on in Houston*
- **Voice in the store:** the same as online. Soft, specific, honest about fit ("this one runs long, petite girls size down"). Name the fabric only when it's confirmed (never "silk" unless verified).

## The first things a customer sees

1. **Window:** two mannequins in best sellers (Israa + Lujain with a Layla Bisht), one small card: "Fitting rooms inside · Free 14-day fit exchange". Hours. QR to the Ramadan early-access list.
2. **Inside the door, a "feel it" rail:** one of each fabric (crepe, chiffon) with the fabric named on a gold tag. This answers "is it see-through?" before anyone asks.
3. **Fitting room:** a full-length mirror with warm, true-colour light, a hook for a hijab, and a small card: "Ask us to check your length."
4. **Register:** a printed exchange policy (one rule for store and online), a "How did you hear about us?" prompt, and the WhatsApp/SMS list QR.
5. **Bag:** a maroon tissue wrap, the policy card and a "Leave us a Google review" card with a QR code.

## Checks before printing anything new

- Make sure the sign contractor's artwork matches the wordmark exactly, and that the City of Houston sign permit is covered (see ops.md).
- Name the Google Maps listing exactly "Bonita Style" (your brief already points directions there).
