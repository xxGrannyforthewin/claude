Your Owner Checklist, Hiring Playbook, Team Handbook, Training Week slides and the four how-to guides (in Google Drive) already cover hiring, payroll, I-9/W-4, Texas new-hire reporting and closing. This page covers **what those documents don't**, and the board's conditions.

## 1. The daily cycle (👁 = the customer sees it)

1. 9:15 arrive, alarm off, lights. Count the drawer against the opening float on the closing sheet.
2. 9:20 check the floor 👁: best sellers up front, every size of the top 5 styles on the rail, fitting rooms clean, mirror light on, steamer warm.
3. 9:30 pull online orders (Shopify + TikTok Shop) and put them in the back-room bin. **Packing happens in the back room only, before 10:00 or in slow hours, never while a shopper is on the floor.**
4. 10:00 open 👁. Greeting and the first five minutes (Section 4).
5. Midday: TikTok Live from the store on scheduled days (TikTok Live guide). One person stays on the floor during a Live.
6. 👁 Checkout: "How did you hear about us?" goes into the Shopify POS order note, plus a WhatsApp/SMS opt-in (Section 4).
7. 4:00 courier pickup for online parcels (time per the Online orders guide).
8. Close (Closing guide): drawer count, cash drop, sales vs. 10-order target written on the closing sheet, re-fill the floor from the back room.

## 4. Routines (one page each, add to the Training Week slides)

**A. The first five minutes (the board's #1 product condition)**
1. Greet within 10 seconds: "Welcome to Bonita Style. Shopping for something special or just looking?"
2. Ask the occasion and the date ("a wedding in December?"). That's the hook for everything else.
3. Pull 2–3 pieces in her size, best sellers first. Ask her height and say honestly how the piece runs ("this runs long, petite girls size down").
4. Fitting room: offer a matching hijab to try *with* it.
5. Check the colour by the window or under the mirror light. "Does it match what you saw on TikTok?"
6. At the register: "How did you hear about us?" → note. "Can we text you when new pieces arrive before Ramadan?" → phone number.
7. Wrap in tissue, and hand over the exchange-policy card.

**B. Exchange and return policy, one rule for store and online (print at the till)**
Suggested: exchange or store credit within 14 days, unworn with tags. Fit exchanges are free. Final-sale only on items marked so. *(Owner to confirm. A free fit exchange costs little and answers the "will it fit?" objection.)*

**C. Inventory sync.** Store and online share one Shopify inventory. Before Oct 31, scan every piece into the Houston location in Shopify. Set the online channels to sell only from the warehouse location, or hold back a store-only count per size on the top 5 styles, so a TikTok order can't empty the rack a customer drove for.

**D. Complaint.** Listen, don't argue, offer an exchange or credit under the policy. Anything above $100 goes to the owner the same day. Ask happy customers for a Google review. Answer every review within 24 hours.

**E. Weekly reorder (Mondays).** Shopify sales by product, last 7 days, store + online. Anything with under 2 of a size left on a top-10 style gets re-ordered or moved from online stock.

## 6. Licences, permits and insurance (confirm each with the office or your attorney/CPA; rules change and this isn't legal advice)

- [ ] **Texas Sales and Use Tax Permit.** Free, and required before the first sale. Your sheet shows it **unpaid/not done**. Apply at comptroller.texas.gov. Houston-area rate is 8.25%, collected on top of prices.
- [ ] **City of Houston Certificate of Occupancy.** Your sheet shows the $200 occupancy permit **unpaid**. Houston Permitting Center (houstonpermittingcenter.org). It usually needs a passed inspection before you open to the public.
- [ ] **Sign permit** for the $10,000 sign. City of Houston sign administration. Check with Arif (sign contractor) whether he pulls it.
- [ ] **Bonita Style LLC registration in Texas.** The plan of merger into the California LLC is in draft. Your checklist already asks the attorney about Form 304. Make sure the sales tax permit, lease and bank account are under the entity that survives.
- [ ] General liability / property insurance for the store (the sheet shows $25/month insurance. Check it covers the Houston location and its inventory).
- [ ] Workers' comp decision + TDI notices (on your checklist).
